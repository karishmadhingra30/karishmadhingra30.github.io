THE STORY

The hardest part of building automation solutions for large enterprises was never the technology; it was understanding what the person at the end of the process actually needed.

I spent years doing that for pharma clients and state agencies, and it's what brought me to UC Berkeley's School of Information, where I'm still chasing the same question, just in higher-stakes contexts. Building HIPAA-compliant AI workflows for clinicians. Prototyping a chatbot to support officials during disaster response. Figuring out where AI is genuinely useful and where it's technically impressive but faces friction in the real world.

What stays consistent: thinking in systems, asking the right questions, and caring whether the solution actually works for the person on the other end.
