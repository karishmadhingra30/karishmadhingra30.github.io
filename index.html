How I work

The details are the product.

01

Start with a human constraint.

Access, time pressure, cognitive load, privacy, and local infrastructure change what “useful” means.

02

Make uncertainty visible.

Strong systems distinguish a known answer from a missing data point, a prototype, or a decision that needs a person.

03

Leave evidence behind.

Clear setup, evaluation results, documented trade-offs, and honest limitations make a project easier to trust and extend.
